# Advocate Digital Presence & Marketing Automation Platform

## Software Requirements Specification (SRS) + Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Version:** 1.0
**Project Type:** Legal Services Website + Digital Marketing + Social Media Automation
**Primary Service:** Will Drafting / Will Registration Assistance
**Location:** Kavali, Andhra Pradesh, India
**Primary Technology Focus:** Website, SEO, Payment Gateway, Secure Document Upload, n8n, Postiz, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Social Media APIs
**Document Status:** Initial Project Specification

---

# 1. Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to build a complete digital presence and automated digital marketing ecosystem for an Advocate based in Kavali, Andhra Pradesh.

The platform will primarily focus on **Will-related legal services**, positioning Will creation as a preventive and responsible legal step that families should consider during their lifetime rather than something that is only addressed after a dispute occurs.

The system will consist of:

1. A professional legal-services website.
2. Dedicated landing pages for Will-related services.
3. Online enquiry and consultation functionality.
4. Online payment gateway integration.
5. Secure document upload functionality.
6. Search Engine Optimization (SEO).
7. Organic digital marketing.
8. Paid advertising campaigns.
9. Social media presence across six platforms.
10. Automated social-media content distribution.
11. Google Drive-based media management.
12. Google Sheets-based content scheduling.
13. n8n-based workflow automation.
14. Postiz-based social-media publishing/scheduling infrastructure.
15. Analytics and reporting.

The long-term objective is not simply to create a website, but to build a **repeatable lead-generation and digital marketing system** that can generate awareness, enquiries, consultations and clients for the legal practice.

The primary business objective is to increase awareness and generate qualified enquiries for the Advocate's legal services, with **Will drafting and related services as the primary marketing focus**.

# 20. Automation Safety / Idempotency

The automation must prevent duplicate posting.

Each platform should have an independent status.

Example:

| Video   | Instagram | Facebook  | YouTube | LinkedIn  | X       | Telegram  |
| ------- | --------- | --------- | ------- | --------- | ------- | --------- |
| Will-01 | Published | Published | Failed  | Published | Pending | Published |

If YouTube fails, the workflow should be able to retry YouTube without reposting the video everywhere else.

# 27. Development Philosophy

The project will follow these principles:

### 1. Reuse before rebuilding

Use existing open-source functionality wherever practical.

### 2. Configure before modifying

Do not modify Postiz unless existing functionality/API cannot satisfy the requirement.

### 3. Modular architecture

Each component should have a clear responsibility.

### 4. Automation with safeguards

Automation should include:

* Logging
* Retry
* Error handling
* Duplicate prevention
* Human approval where appropriate

### 5. Security first

Legal documents and client information are sensitive and require appropriate protection.

### 6. Incremental development

Build and test one component at a time rather than attempting the entire system simultaneously.
