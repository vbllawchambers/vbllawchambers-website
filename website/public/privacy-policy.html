Privacy Policy

VBL Law Chambers & Advocate Social Automation Suite | Last Updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

VBL Law Chambers ("we", "our", or "us"), founded in 1999 and operating from Kavali, SPSR Nellore District, Andhra Pradesh, India, owns and manages the website https://vbllawchambers.com along with its advocate workflow automation tools and applications (collectively referred to as the "App", "VBL Law Chambers", or "VBL Social Automation").

We hold user privacy in the highest regard. This Privacy Policy details the types of information we collect, how it is processed and protected, and our stringent compliance with international data privacy standards, the Bar Council of India rules, and Google API Services User Data Policies.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information only to the extent necessary to deliver professional legal advisory services and manage authorized public outreach:

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you submit a legal consultation query through our contact form, we collect your name, phone number, email address, practice area of interest, and case summary.
  • Automated Device & Browsing Data: Standard server logs including IP address, browser type, referral links, and timestamps to maintain site security and uptime.
  • Social Platform Integration Credentials: For authorized firm administrators scheduling educational awareness videos and legal updates across verified official channels (such as YouTube, Meta, and Instagram).

3. Google API Services & YouTube Data Policy Compliance

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

Our application integrates with Google API Services (specifically the YouTube Data API v3) to enable authorized firm administrators to upload, schedule, and publish educational legal awareness videos to the official VBL Law Chambers YouTube channel (@vbllawchambers).

Limited Use Disclosure: VBL Law Chambers' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • Data Accessed: We request only the minimal scopes required to manage video uploads (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). We do not access, read, or harvest user personal profiles, contacts, emails, or personal Google Drive files.
  • No Sale or Third-Party Sharing: We do not sell, rent, lease, or transfer any data obtained through Google APIs to any third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • Secure Local Storage: OAuth tokens and refresh credentials are stored in encrypted, password-protected local credential stores solely for executing scheduled publish tasks.
  • User Revocation: Users and administrators can revoke access to their Google account at any time via Google Security Settings (Third-party apps with account access).
  • Google Privacy Policy: For more details on Google's data practices, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Collected Information

We process collected data exclusively for:

  • Reviewing and responding to legal inquiries and scheduling confidential consultations.
  • Delivering legal representation, advisory, and document drafting once formally engaged.
  • Executing authorized automated publishing of educational content to verified law firm social channels.
  • Maintaining website security and complying with statutory obligations in India.

5. Advocate-Client Privilege & Professional Confidentiality

In accordance with the Indian Evidence Act, 1872, and the professional code of conduct established by the Bar Council of India, all privileged communications, documents, and records shared with our advocates are maintained with the strictest confidentiality and are protected by attorney-client privilege.

6. Data Security & Retention

We employ robust industry-standard safeguards, including HTTPS/TLS 256-bit encryption for all data in transit, strict access control lists, and encrypted authentication tokens. Information submitted for initial inquiries is retained only as long as necessary to evaluate the inquiry or fulfill statutory compliance requirements.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal contact records stored with us. You may exercise these rights at any time by sending a written request to our designated privacy contact.
